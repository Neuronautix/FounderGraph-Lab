-Goal-
Given a text document that is potentially relevant to a startup or innovation project, identify all entities that match the following types and all relationships between those entities.

-Steps-
1. Identify all entities. For each identified entity, extract the following information:
- entity_name: Name of the entity, capitalized
- entity_type: One of the following types: [{entity_types}]
- entity_description: Comprehensive description of the entity's attributes and activities

Format each entity as ("entity"{tuple_delimiter}<entity_name>{tuple_delimiter}<entity_type>{tuple_delimiter}<entity_description>)

Entity type definitions:
- CustomerSegment: A specific group of customers or users the startup targets
- Problem: A pain point, challenge or unmet need experienced by customers
- ValueProposition: The core value, benefit or solution the startup offers
- ProductFeature: A specific capability, module or feature of the product
- Assumption: An unvalidated belief or hypothesis that the business depends on
- Evidence: Data, research, quotes or observations that support or challenge assumptions
- Risk: A threat, uncertainty or failure mode that could harm the business
- Experiment: A test, pilot or validation study designed to learn something
- Decision: A strategic or product decision that has been made
- Milestone: A key achievement, launch date or target outcome
- GrantCall: A funding opportunity, grant program or competition
- Investor: A person or fund that has invested or may invest capital
- Partner: An organization collaborating with or enabling the startup
- Competitor: An existing solution or company competing for the same customers
- IPAsset: Intellectual property such as patents, trade secrets or proprietary data
- RegulatoryConstraint: A law, regulation or compliance requirement
- TechnicalDependency: An external technology, API, platform or library the product relies on
- FinancialHypothesis: An assumption about revenue, cost, unit economics or financial viability

2. From the entities identified in step 1, identify all pairs of (source_entity, target_entity) that are *clearly related* to each other.
For each pair of related entities, extract the following information:
- source_entity: name of the source entity, as identified in step 1
- target_entity: name of the target entity, as identified in step 1
- relationship_description: explanation as to why you think the source entity and the target entity are related to each other
- relationship_strength: a numeric score indicating strength of the relationship between the source entity and target entity

Format each relationship as ("relationship"{tuple_delimiter}<source_entity>{tuple_delimiter}<target_entity>{tuple_delimiter}<relationship_description>{tuple_delimiter}<relationship_strength>)

3. Return output in English as a single list of all the entities and relationships identified in steps 1 and 2. Use **{record_delimiter}** as the list delimiter.

4. When finished, output {completion_delimiter}
