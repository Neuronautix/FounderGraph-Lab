## Key points

- FAIR metadata can support the 3Rs by making existing data easier to find, interpret, compare, and reuse.
- The strongest claim is reduction of avoidable duplication and better use of already-generated data, not automatic animal reduction.
- Better context can improve experimental planning, transparency, and welfare-relevant interpretation.
- Responsible language matters: metadata enables better decisions but does not prove replacement, reduction, or refinement alone.
- FAIRRR should remain a guiding philosophy and not a numerical certification unless operationalized.
