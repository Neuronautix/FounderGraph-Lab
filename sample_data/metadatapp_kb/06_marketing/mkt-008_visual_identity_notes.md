## Purpose

- This file is part of a synthetic but realistic Metadatapp knowledge base for a mock knowledge-graph experiment.
- It is point-ended by design: short claims, explicit entities, explicit relations, and open questions are easier to extract into a graph.
- Client and partner situations are fictionalized unless described as standards, technologies, or general market concepts.

## Key points

- Use dark backgrounds with high-contrast scientific UI elements.
- Use visual metaphor: fragmented documents converging into a structured graph.
- Use modular cards for protocol, animals, cage, endpoints, events, validation, and export.
- Avoid generic stock laboratory clichés.
- Avoid overpromising AI or 3Rs impact visually.
- The brand should communicate infrastructure, trust, and scientific rigor.
