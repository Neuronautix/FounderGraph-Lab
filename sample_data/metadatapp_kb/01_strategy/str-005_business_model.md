## Key points

- Open-source core can build trust and academic adoption.
- Cloud subscription can monetize hosting, updates, team workflows, validation reports, and standard exports.
- Enterprise/private deployment can monetize security, SSO, custom connectors, and support SLA.
- Professional services can monetize metadata schema design, FAIR audit, ontology mapping, pilot deployment, and training.
- Premium modules can include connector packs, governance dashboards, DOI/RO-Crate automation, and VCG-readiness reports.
