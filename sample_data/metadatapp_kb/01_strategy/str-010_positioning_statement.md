## Key points

- For preclinical teams generating complex animal research data, Metadatapp is a FAIR-by-design metadata orchestration layer.
- It makes experimental context structured, validated, interoperable, and reusable.
- Unlike generic ELNs, spreadsheets, or vendor-specific tools, it focuses on cross-system protocol metadata and semantic export.
- The core tagline can be: data are not reusable until context is computable.
- The product should speak operationally first and semantically second.
