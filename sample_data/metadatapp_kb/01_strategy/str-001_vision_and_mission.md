## Purpose

- This file is part of a synthetic but realistic Metadatapp knowledge base for a mock knowledge-graph experiment.
- It is point-ended by design: short claims, explicit entities, explicit relations, and open questions are easier to extract into a graph.
- Client and partner situations are fictionalized unless described as standards, technologies, or general market concepts.

## Key points

- Metadatapp exists to make experimental context computable before data become unusable.
- The mission is to help preclinical teams capture, validate, connect, and reuse metadata around animal experiments.
- The ethical hypothesis is that better metadata can reduce avoidable duplication and improve interpretation.
- The strategic claim must remain modest: metadata infrastructure enables better 3Rs decisions but does not prove animal reduction alone.
- Metadatapp should be positioned as a context layer, not as another isolated database.
