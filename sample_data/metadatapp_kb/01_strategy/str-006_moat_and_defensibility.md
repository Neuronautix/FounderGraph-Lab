## Key points

- A generic form builder is not a defensible moat.
- A prompt-only AI curation tool is not defensible without validation and provenance.
- Defensibility can come from domain-specific metadata templates, ontology mappings, SHACL shapes, connector workflows, and scientific trust.
- The HCM/behavior niche creates founder-market fit and differentiated domain knowledge.
- The long-term moat is accumulated semantic mapping and workflow knowledge across preclinical studies.
